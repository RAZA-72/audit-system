The AuditIQ Standard

A scoring methodology, not a guess

AuditIQ is an independent auditing standard, built and maintained by Milleniance Softnet Pvt Ltd. Every score is produced the same way, every time — so a 92 means the same thing whether it's your site or a competitor's.

1

Real engines, not heuristics

Speed and UI/UX checks run on the same Lighthouse and axe-core engines used by web performance teams worldwide — not a proprietary black box.

2

Server-verified, not simulated

Security checks read your live SSL/TLS certificate directly; SEO checks parse your actual sitemap and robots.txt — nothing is inferred.

3

Weighted by real-world impact

Issues are ranked by how much they cost you in visitors or conversions — not just how many boxes are unchecked.
